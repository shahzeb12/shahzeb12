## 🚀 About Me

I'm a **Senior Full-Stack Developer** who enjoys building software that turns complex business processes into simple, efficient digital experiences.

My primary focus is developing **web portals, SaaS platforms, dashboards, CRM integrations, and business automation systems** that help organizations reduce administrative overhead and manage their operations more effectively.

I work across the entire application stack — from intuitive React interfaces and robust Laravel APIs to databases, third-party integrations, and AWS infrastructure.

> **My goal:** Build software that solves real business problems, not just software that looks good.
